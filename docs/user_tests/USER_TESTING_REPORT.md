# User Testing Feedback

## Focused User Test Questionnaire

The following questionnaire was sent to both clients.

### Instructions

1. Navigate to [https://esof423.csit.help/](https://esof423.csit.help/) in a web browser.
2. Use either the “Find Company Filings” or “Recent Filings” functionality to find a company or specific filing.
3. Run a new analysis or view an existing analysis (there are existing analyses for PEPSICO INC).

### Questions

1. If you experienced any notable bugs or glitches during the steps above:
   - 1a. Was it with the “Find Company Filings” / search functionality? Explain your experience.
   - 1b. Was it with the “Recent Filings” functionality? Explain your experience.
   - 1c. Was it with the page of an individual company (like PEPSICO INC)? Explain your experience.
   - 1d. Was it with the “Open Original” document in a new tab? Explain your experience.
   - 1e. Was it with the “Sentiment” functionality? Explain your experience.
   - 1f. Was it with the “Run Report” functionality? Explain your experience.
   - 1g. Were there discrepancies between functionality in English and German?
   - 1h. Were there unexpected changes in functionality between the U.S. and German filing markets?
   - 1i. Were there general performance issues, visual impediments, or unclear areas of the application?
2. How would you describe our website to another person in your field?
3. In a finished state where financial analysis jobs could run much faster (for example, on a dedicated AI workstation), could you see this website as a useful tool while working in a finance-related career?
4. What were some things you liked about the website?
5. Was there anything you disliked about the website?
6. Do you want to provide any additional feedback or suggestions for the authors?

## INDEPENDENT USER TEST

### USER 1: “M” — family member

The family member had difficulty using the Recent Filings area. They were unsure what to choose for filing type and reporting year, and the finance terminology was unfamiliar to them.

After searching for a company, they were also confused by the filing-selection interface and how to proceed with the filings. This test surfaced uncertainty both in getting started and in understanding the choices presented after a company search.

They liked the site’s styling choices but felt the website was too confusing to use.

**Follow-up status:** Since this test, the filing-selection interface has been simplified in the local project version. That local update has not yet been merged into the hosted version, so the hosted experience may still differ.

## FOCUSED USER TEST

During the week of November 9th, we demoed the current iteration of our software to two clients.

### CLIENT 1: “W”

Client 1, “W,” reported no issues in the test process and was able to generate a report about Apple.

They described our application as:

> “An efficient way to collect data about companies that could be influential for picking stocks. I think it could be huge for people who pick their own stocks because they are able to pore through more real data than before. It reminds me of a Bloomberg terminal that isn't insanely expensive but is missing some functions of a Bloomberg. I like how easy the website is to navigate and how straightforward it is.”

### CLIENT 2: “J”

Client 2, “J,” encountered usability issues immediately after opening the website. It was not apparent to them what the website did or how to use it.

After minimal intervention, they were able to scroll through Recent Filings, filter results, and eventually search for companies.

They were unsure what happened after pressing “Run Report.” They also noted that the current wait times made a disclaimer or progress indicator important.

## Conclusion

The tests surfaced different experiences: one client completed the report flow without difficulty, while the other client and the family member had trouble understanding parts of the interface. Their observations give the team concrete usability issues to consider in the next iteration.
